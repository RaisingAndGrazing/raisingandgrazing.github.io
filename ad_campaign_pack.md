## Etsy Ads test structure

- Test 3 listing clusters separately: digital wall art, practical gifts (mugs/totes), and identity humor (stickers/apparel).
- Start with the strongest 3–5 listings per cluster rather than advertising all 27 listings.
- Keep one control creative per cluster for 7 days before changing copy.
- Pause any listing that gets clicks but no favorites or carts after a meaningful sample; improve the listing photo before increasing spend.

## Creative rules

- Show the product large enough to understand on a phone.
- Use warm cream, charcoal, muted sage, and terracotta accents.
- Keep text short and leave clear space for the platform overlay.
- Do not use placeholder artwork, invented reviews, fake urgency, or unsupported claims.
