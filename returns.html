Returns & Refunds

Effective date: August 1, 2026

Every Raising & Grazing item is made to order and printed just for you by our print partner. Because each piece is produced individually, we handle returns a little differently than a mass-retail shop — but we stand fully behind the quality of what we send.

Return Window

If there is a problem with your order, please contact us within 30 days of delivery so we can make it right.

Eligible for a Free Replacement or Full Refund

We will replace the item or issue a full refund — at no cost to you — if your order arrives:

  • Damaged in transit.
  • Defective or misprinted (printing errors, flaws, wrong colors).
  • The wrong item, size, or design from what you ordered.

Please include a clear photo of the issue when you contact us — it lets us resolve it fast and send a replacement or refund right away. You do not need to ship the item back for these cases.

Not Eligible

Because items are custom-made on demand, we cannot accept returns or exchanges for:

  • Buyer's remorse or a change of mind.
  • Ordering the wrong size or option (please check sizing before ordering — we're happy to help if you ask first).
  • Instant digital download products, which are non-returnable once purchased.

How to Request a Return, Replacement, or Refund

Message us through our Etsy shop (etsy.com/shop/RaisingAndGrazing) or email RaisingAndGrazing@gmail.com with your order number and a photo of any issue. We typically respond within 1–2 business days.

Refund Method & Timing

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method. Once approved, refunds are processed promptly and typically appear within 3–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.

Return Shipping

For eligible damaged, defective, or incorrect items, you pay nothing — no return shipping is required. If a return is ever requested and approved, we will provide instructions.

Questions

We're a small shop and we genuinely want you happy with your order. Reach us any time through our Etsy shop or at RaisingAndGrazing@gmail.com.
