Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

Raising & Grazing (“we,” “us,” “our”) operates an online shop selling print-on-demand products through Etsy, and uses social platforms including Pinterest to promote our products. This policy explains what information we handle and how.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information through this policy’s associated application. When you purchase from our shop, the following may be processed to fulfill your order:

  • Order details you provide to Etsy — your name, shipping address, and email address.
  • Transaction information — items ordered, order totals, and shipping status.

We do not collect payment card details; payments are handled securely by Etsy.

How We Use Information

We use order information only to:

  • Produce, fulfill, and ship your order (via our print partner).
  • Provide customer support and respond to your messages.
  • Comply with legal and tax obligations.

Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted third parties, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Etsy — our marketplace and checkout provider. See Etsy’s Privacy Policy.
  • Printify — our print-on-demand and fulfillment partner; receives the shipping details needed to produce and deliver your order.
  • Pinterest — used to publish and promote our product listings. Our use of the Pinterest API is limited to posting content to our own Pinterest business account; we do not collect personal data from Pinterest users.

Sharing of Information

We share order information only with the partners above as needed to fulfill orders, and when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Retention

We retain order information for as long as needed to fulfill orders, provide support, and meet legal, accounting, and reporting requirements.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or object to its processing. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Cookies & Tracking

This policy’s associated application does not set cookies. Purchases and browsing on Etsy and Pinterest are subject to those platforms’ own cookie and privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

Our shop is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Effective date” above reflects the most recent revision.
