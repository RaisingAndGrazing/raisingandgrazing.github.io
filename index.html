Our story

Made with care, one order at a time

Raising & Grazing started as a home-and-table brand built around one idea: the things you live with every day should feel warm, personal and a little bit funny. We design each piece ourselves and produce it only when it's ordered, through trusted print-on-demand partners in the United States. You can browse and buy the full collection on our Etsy shop, and follow along on our blog for gift guides and new releases.
